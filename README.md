### 👨‍💻 A little about the human behind the commits

I'm a software engineer from **Barcelona** with around **6 years of experience**, mainly in frontend development—and a growing curiosity for everything behind the screen.

- 🛠️ My home turf is **TypeScript, Angular, React, and Vue**, with experience across backend services, databases, and delivery workflows.
